# Brand Tier Interest Signals Pageviews 12m News Search
Method. Interest blends Wikipedia sitelink breadth with an attention composite of all-language pageviews, GDELT news volume and Google Trends, each square-root-scaled to its top-5 mean and combined over the sources available per brand. Top-5 average = 100. No figure is estimated. Sources: Wikipedia, GDELT, Google Trends, Wikidata. Not investment advice.
